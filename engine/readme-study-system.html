Read me

This exam has two pages that work as one system: the Drill finds what you do not know and proves when you do, and the Mastery Guide teaches it again. Every fact on a Fix my misses card in the Drill is in the Guide section it links to.

How to use the Drill

  1. Start with the pre-test. It asks 3 questions from every topic and gives each topic a level: Strong (80% and up), Okay (60-79%) or Weak (under 60%). It never uses last-pass questions, so those stay fresh.
  2. Or go lecture by lecture. The "By lecture" card gives every lecture its own pre-test (up to 8 questions), its own last pass, a level, and a Guide button that opens only that lecture's sections.
  3. Think first. Each question appears without its choices for 10-15 seconds. Answer it in your head before the choices show up. Turn the timer off on any question if you need to.
  4. Be honest after a right answer. Choose "Knew it" or "I guessed" (key G). A guess comes back sooner.
  5. Read the "why" on every miss. You get why your pick is wrong, the key idea and the concept card. A different question on the same concept follows 3-6 questions later.
  6. Use short daily sessions. Pick 20 questions or a 30-minute timed plan. Questions you just answered rest before they come back, so each session is new work.
  7. Finish with the last pass. It asks every question from the cases asked in class, the slide exercises and the facts flagged on slides, shakiest first.

Keys: 1-6 pick an option, Enter checks and moves on, S stars, G marks a guess. Progress saves on this device and, inside claude.ai, to your account so your phone, tablet and laptop share it.

How to use the Mastery Guide

  1. Use the lecture bar at the top to show one lecture at a time, or "All lectures" for everything.
  2. Read a section in order: how it works, what to do first, the client example, the common trap and the memory trick.
  3. Cover the page and tap each blank in "Test yourself" only after you have said the answer.
  4. Finish each section by reading the Key ideas box. Those lines are exactly what the Drill's concept cards show.

How to use both together

  1. Drill to find: run the pre-test, then open Fix my misses to see the concepts you keep missing.
  2. Guide to re-learn: follow the card's "Open in the guide" link and study that section.
  3. Drill to prove: come back and press "Fix this". A question counts as solid only after you get it right twice in a row (three times for true/false), with rest between.
